\ignore Some instructors may not want to distribute any of these solutions to students;
other instructors may want to save class time by distributing to students solutions to some of the exercises.  Obviously students
should not see the solution to an exercise until they have attempted the
exercise themselves.  \textit{\textbf{Please do not distribute solutions needlessly, especially if there is a danger that solutions will get passed down to next year's class at your university.}}  Neither I nor Springer will distribute this Instructor's Solutions Manual to students, so if neither you

reasonably confident that your students are working the exercises without benefit
of these solutions.
