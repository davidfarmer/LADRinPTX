
\exercise
Show that $\al + \beta = \beta + \al$ for all $\al, \beta \in \C{}$.


\exercise
Show that $(\al + \beta) +  \la = \al + (\beta + \la)$ for all $\al, \beta, \la \in \C{}$.


\exercise
Show that $(\al \beta)\la = \al(\beta \la)$ for all $\al, \beta, \la \in \C{}$.


\exercise
Show that $\la(\al + \beta) = \la \al + \la \beta$ for all $\la, \al, \beta \in
\C{}$.


\exercise
Show that for every $\al \in \C{}$, there exists a unique $\beta \in \C{}$ such that
$\al + \beta = 0$.


\exercise
Show that for every $\al \in \C{}$ with $\al \ne 0$, there exists a unique $\beta \in \C{}$ such that $\al \beta = 1$.


\exercise
Show that
\[
\frac{-1 + \sqrt{3} \, i}{2}
\]
is a cube root of $1$ (meaning that its cube  equals~$1$).


\exercise
Find two distinct square roots of $i$.


\exercise
Find $x \in \R{4}$ such that
\[
(4, -3, 1, 7) + 2x = (5, 9, -6, 8).
\]
\exercisedisplayend


\exercise
Explain why there does not exist $\la \in \C{}$ such that
\[
\la (2 - 3i, 5 + 4i, -6 + 7i) = (12 - 5i, 7 + 22i, -32 - 9i).
\]
\exercisedisplayend


\exercise
Show that $(x + y) + z  = x + (y + z) $ for all $x, y, z \in \FF{n}$.


\exercise
Show that $(ab)x = a(bx)$ for all $x \in \F{n}$ and all $a, b \in \F{}\3$.


\exercise
Show that $1x = x$ for all $x \in \FF{n}$.


\exercise
Show that $\la(x + y) = \la x + \la y$ for all $\la \in \F{}$ and all $x, y \in \FF{n}$.


\exercise
Show that $(a + b)x = ax + bx$ for all $a, b \in \F{}$ and all $x \in \FF{n}$.
